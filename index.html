Legacy United • Student & User Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Version 2.4

1. Institutional Commitment & Legal Entity

Legacy United ("Legacy United", "we", "our", or "us"), operating under official corporate domain legacyunited.io, maintains ironclad standards of student, educator, and guardian privacy. This Privacy Policy governs all data collected across our physical learning academies, educational testing centers, and sovereign digital EdTech mobile applications on iOS, iPadOS, and the web.

2. Strict Compliance with Student Privacy Laws (COPPA, FERPA, GDPR)

Legacy United complies fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Zero Advertising & Tracking: We never display third-party advertisements, sell student personal information, or utilize commercial behavioral tracking SDKs.
  • Data Minimization: We collect only information strictly necessary to deliver academic curricula, administer coursework, and issue certified completion records.
  • Parental Rights: Parents and legal guardians possess the absolute right to inspect, correct, or demand the permanent deletion of their student's personal information at any time.

3. Categories of Information Collected

  • Account Information: Student/guardian name, institutional email address, school affiliation, and enrolled academic grades.
  • Curriculum & Learning Telemetry: Lesson progress, test evaluations, laboratory assignment submissions, and instructor feedback logs.
  • Technical Diagnostics: Device model, OS build version, and anonymous crash telemetry utilized strictly to optimize software reliability and performance on Apple devices.

4. Data Security, Retention & Encryption Standards

All student records and data transmissions are encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS 1.3 and encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption on secure, SOC-2 audited enterprise infrastructure. Data is retained only for the active duration of the student's academic enrollment plus any legally required archival periods.

5. Account Deletion & Right to Erasure (Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v))

In accordance with Apple App Store Review Guidelines, users and guardians may request complete and permanent account deletion and erasure of all associated academic records directly from their mobile application profile settings or by submitting an email to: support@legacyunited.io. Account deletion requests are executed within 7 business days.
